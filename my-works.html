My Work

I have revolutionized the industry by deploying AI-driven architecture, where buildings self-optimize for sustainability and adapt in real-time to their occupants' needs. I’ve pioneered the creation of 3D-printed smart homes that feature fully reconfigurable interiors and embedded intelligence, seamlessly integrating with the digital ecosystem. These innovations redefine construction and interior design, making futuristic living a present-day reality.

Underwater Megastructure

The Underwater Megastructure provides sustainable oceanic habitats with panoramic views, built using advanced robotics and eco-friendly technology. This project harmonizes human habitation with the marine ecosystem, expanding living spaces beneath the sea.

Vertical Farm Towers

The Vertical Farm Towers enable year-round urban agriculture by stacking lush, multi-layered crops within towering skyscrapers. Utilizing automated systems for irrigation and harvesting, these towers bring sustainable food production directly into city environments, reducing land use and enhancing food security.
